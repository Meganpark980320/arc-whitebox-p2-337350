# Inventory of fitted and hand-set parts

**Symbols used below.** Layers are ℓ = 0…15 (ℓ = 0 is the first hidden layer, ℓ = 15 the last); n = 1024 is the width. F is the FLOP count billed for one predict, and C = max(0.1, F/2^41) is the compute fraction. MSE is the final-layer mean squared error against Monte Carlo truth. κ3 and κ4 are the third and fourth cumulants of the pre-activations. λ_ℓ is the scalar that regenerates the off-diagonal κ4 core at layer ℓ as λ_ℓ times the covariance. α = μ/σ is a neuron's standardised pre-activation mean. A strength τ multiplies a derived correction, so τ = 1 is its derived value. "Inside" means a part changes a quantity that layer ℓ + 1 reads; "output" means it changes only the returned means.

**Summary.** The skeleton of the estimator, ARC's cumulant propagation, is derived, and so are our supply terms (§4) and the depth analysis (§§2–3). The shipped output also rests on about 55,000 fitted constants (the rows above, without the inert C4 table) and about ten hand-set strengths, some of them chosen on held-out networks that we went on to reuse. The inherited κ4 constant λ runs at two to five times its physical value and, by the code's own account, works partly by cancelling a truncation error. Four of our derived strengths ship at tuned values: O1 1.5, K31F 1.1, C2E 0.65 and feedback-birth 0.38–0.41, against derived values of 1, 1, 1 and ½. What these parts carry is measured in the write-up's §5 (Tables 5.2–5.3). Switching off every switchable fitted part raises the final-layer MSE by 78–82 %, and the inherited λ at a physical value alone raises it by 71 %.
